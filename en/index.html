About AIRS

Bringing RoboCup-honed technology and a spirit of challenge to the real world.

With a core of members active at the RoboCup world championship in service robotics, AIRS works on research, development, and real-world deployment in robotics and AI.

From curiosity to implementation.

We learn about new research and technology early and try it with our own hands. From AI to humanoids, we expand what we can do through repeated implementation and validation.

Services

Robotics and automation

We automate transport, assembly, and inspection with robots, from development and deployment to operation and maintenance.

AI and software development

We develop and maintain AI and machine learning software tailored to your operations.

Research and technical support

In robotics and AI, we provide contract research and technical consulting, and support the development of engineers and students through education and training.

Achieving point-cloud mapping and navigation over 100 m × 100 m with Unitree Go2, without additional hardware

The LiDAR built into Unitree Go2 could not produce an accurate map on its own. Our own system solved this, achieving point-cloud mapping and navigation over a 100 m × 100 m area without additional hardware.

Teaching a dual-arm robot to fold a handkerchief

Trained on 100 demonstrations, ACT folded a handkerchief into quarters in 37 of 40 trials under the same cloth and placement conditions. Failures with different patterns or positions show the limits we are investigating on the real robot.

From Replicating RL Token to Reward Design with Humans and AI

An experiment teaching a robot to move oranges onto a plate revealed that its success metric measured proximity instead. Human video review led to revised success criteria, rewards, and AI audit questions. Here is what was verified and what remains unresolved.

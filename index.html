About us (English)

AI Robot Systems LLC is a company based in Kanazawa, Ishikawa, Japan, founded on September 1, 2026. We provide system integration, development and operational support for autonomous mobile robots and robot-arm manipulation systems; development of AI and machine-learning software; automation of patrol, monitoring and inspection of industrial facilities; and related research and development, contract research, technical consulting, and training for engineers and students.
